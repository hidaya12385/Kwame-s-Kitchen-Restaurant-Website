Our Story

Cooking that started at home, in Tamale

Kwame's Kitchen began as Sunday lunches for the neighborhood — big pots of jollof, banku on the fire, whoever was around got a plate. Word spread, and eventually the Sunday table became a full kitchen.

Every recipe on our menu still traces back to that table: my grandmother's pepper sauce, my mother's waakye stew, my own additions from years of experimenting on customers who kept coming back for more.

We buy from local market vendors every morning, so what's on your plate depends a little on what was freshest that day — and we like it that way.
